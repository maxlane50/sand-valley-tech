Design three mobile screens for a private golf trip scoreboard — 7 friends,
4 rounds at Sand Valley Resort in Wisconsin, cumulative net Stableford.
Phone-first; it will be opened on iPhones, occasionally on a laptop.

Screen 1 — Leaderboard. 7 players ranked by cumulative Stableford points.
Each row: position, name, total points, points-back-from-leader, and a
small per-round breakdown (4 round scores). The leader should feel
obviously like the leader. Rows need to be readable at a glance on a
phone at a bar.

Screen 2 — Score entry. One player's 18 holes at a time. Big numeric
keypad, auto-advance to the next hole, a strip showing the 18 holes
with pars and which ones this player gets a stroke on. A running total
at the top. Needs to be fast — I'm entering 126 numbers after each round.

Screen 3 — Stats. Everything here derives from ONE input: gross strokes
per hole. No putts, no fairways, no GIR — do not design anything that
needs data beyond strokes, par, and hole stroke index.

Aesthetic: links golf, sand dunes, fescue. Restrained, not a corporate
sports app, not neon. Real typographic personality. Think a well-made
printed scorecard rather than a fantasy-football dashboard.

Give me the design tokens (colors, type scale, spacing) as CSS variables
alongside the screens so I can hand them to an engineer.

Show:
- Each player's scoring average vs par on par 3s, par 4s, par 5s, and
  Stableford points earned per hole type. This is the centrepiece —
  it should make it instantly obvious who's the par-5 assassin and who
  can't hit a green on a par 3.
- Group-wide hardest and easiest holes across the trip (actual scoring
  average vs the card's stroke index — the fun is where they disagree).
- Birdie / par / bogey / blow-up counts per player.
- A few earned superlatives: most points in a single round, longest
  streak of holes with points, most blow-ups.

Tone: the leaderboard is the official record, this screen is the
argument at the bar. It can be looser and more playful, but it still
has to be legible on a phone and consistent with the same design tokens.
